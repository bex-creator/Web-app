Cloud Browser Standby

Enter your Hyperbeam API key in the top bar above to start your 7-hour daily cloud session.

How It Works

  1. Paste your key into the top field and click Start Session.
  2. Your key is saved locally in your browser so you don't need to re-type it.
  3. The 7-hour timer runs only while your browser session is active.
  4. Your daily allowance automatically resets at midnight every day.
